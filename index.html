How to read this

beginner guide
Price / ¢ — a contract pays $1 if it wins. Price 60¢ ≈ the market thinks there's a 60% chance.
American odds — -150 means risk $150 to win $100 (favorite). +150 means risk $100 to win $150 (underdog).
% of volume — how big the bet is compared with all money ever traded in that market. Big % = real conviction, not noise (threshold 5%).
Held? — does the whale still hold the position now (✅ = at least 80% still in). Sold quickly = noise.
Edge — whale's average price minus today's Kalshi price for the same side. Green / positive = you can buy on Kalshi at the same price or cheaper than the whale paid. It is NOT a guarantee of profit — whales lose too.
Score (0-100) — 25 size vs volume, 15 still held, 10 timing (entered ≥1h early / price moved their way), 20 Kalshi price vs whale entry, 20 whale wallet's all-time profit, 10 resolves soon.
